# MoreClipz Clipper: Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 18, 2026_

MoreClipz Clipper is a personal tool used only by its operator to post their own livestream clips.

**What it accesses**, only for accounts the operator connects:
- Basic profile: display name, avatar and account ID.
- Sign-in tokens issued by the platform, so it can upload on the operator's behalf.
- Statistics for videos it posted: views, likes, comments, shares and, where offered, watch time.

**What it does with it.** The data is used only to post the operator's clips and show how they performed.
It is stored on the operator's own computer, and sign-in tokens are stored encrypted. Nothing is sold,
shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

**Viewers.** The app does not collect any information about people who watch the posted videos.

**Deleting data.** Disconnecting an account in the app deletes its stored tokens. Access can also be revoked
at any time in the platform's own settings (for TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions).

**Changes.** This policy may be updated; the date above shows the latest version.
