# MoreClipz Clipper

MoreClipz Clipper is a personal desktop tool run by the operator of the **MoreClipzTV** channel. It watches
livestreams the operator has permission to clip, finds the highlights, cuts them into short vertical videos with
subtitles, and posts them to the operator's own accounts on TikTok, YouTube and Instagram.

**How posting works**

- The operator signs in to each platform with their own account through the platform's official login.
- Every clip waits in a review queue. Nothing is posted until the operator reviews it and presses Post.
- For TikTok, the operator chooses who can see each video, whether comments, Duets and Stitches are allowed, and
  whether the video needs a commercial content disclosure, before it is posted with TikTok's Content Posting API.
- Afterwards the app reads the video's view, like, comment and share counts so the operator can see what works.

The app isn't offered to anyone else, has no user accounts, and doesn't collect anything about viewers.
